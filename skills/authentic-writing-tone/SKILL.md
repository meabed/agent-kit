# Authentic writing tone

1. Identify the audience, purpose, required action, supplied voice samples, and facts supported by
   the source material.
2. Use plain, specific language a real engineer, customer, or operator would use.
3. Avoid generic AI phrasing such as "unlock", "revolutionize", "supercharge", "cutting-edge",
   "seamless", "game-changing", "world-class", "leverage", "delve", and stock conclusions.
4. Do not repeat the same phrase, metaphor, or benefit across multiple products or pages.
5. Do not invent facts, statistics, customers, outcomes, quotes, features, or credentials. Remove
   unsupported claims or flag them when the user requests an audit.
6. Compare alternative wording when the first draft sounds generic. Remove repetition,
   throat-clearing, and claims that do not add specific meaning.
7. Verify every factual claim, product name, link, and number before delivering the final copy.
